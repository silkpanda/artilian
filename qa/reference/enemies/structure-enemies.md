# Structure Enemies — Silhouette & Visual Reference Specs

Reference silhouettes and palettes for the three Phase 2 biome structure guardians (`artilian:structure_enemies`), ensuring each has an unmistakable archetype rather than sharing a generic humanoid clone.

---

## 1. Prism Sentinel (`artilian:prism_sentinel`)
- **Biome / Structure:** Crystal Canopy Woods — **Crystal Grove Shrine**
- **Elements / Hitbox:** `CRYSTAL / EARTH` (`0.95 × 2.05`, `eyeHeight = 1.75F`, `shadowRadius = 0.55F`)
- **Silhouette Archetype:** Towering **Chiseled Temple-Stone & Living Prism-Crystal Monolith Golem** matching the weathered mossy chiseled stone, dark arcane-violet bark, and glowing Starlight-Teal / Amethyst blooms of the Crystal Grove Shrine.
  - **Torso:** Tapered rune-carved stone waist, broad chiseled monolith chest, proud dark-beveled **Prism Heart Core** with multifaceted teal-and-amethyst refraction, angled 3D dorsal crystal spine, and jagged dorsal crystal cluster cutout.
  - **Head & Crest:** Heavy chiseled stone brow overhang, recessed dark visor cavity with twin glowing Starlight-Teal & Amethyst prism eyes + central diamond rune, angled crystal cheek horns, carved back-of-head shrine rune, and a hovering `45°` Y-rotated diamond prism spire flanked by crossed orbiting crystal shard cutouts.
  - **Limbs:** Stepped stone pauldrons crowned with angled 3D shoulder crystal spires + jagged crystal shard fans, stone upper arms, heavy prism-crusher gauntlets with glowing knuckle inlays, and stepped mossy-stone temple greaves with glowing ankle runes.
- **Palette & Glowmask:** Arcane Temple Stone (`#161222` → `#C4B8E0`), Canopy Moss (`#0D3B32` → `#3BC29E`), Starlight-Teal Crystal (`#093844` → `#38D9C0` → `#FFFFFF`), Amethyst Refraction (`#34104C` → `#A855F7` → `#FFFFFF`). Glowmask illuminates visor eyes, Prism Heart Core, hovering crest & orbiting shards, shoulder spires, gauntlet knuckles, dorsal spine, and ankle runes.

## 2. Cinder Raider (`artilian:cinder_raider`)
- **Biome / Structure:** Emberbloom Meadows — **Emberbloom Outpost**
- **Elements / Hitbox:** `FIRE / EARTH` (`0.80 × 1.75`, `eyeHeight = 1.45F`, `shadowRadius = 0.45F`)
- **Silhouette Archetype:** Compact, hunched **Volcanic-Basalt & Forged-Iron Pyro-Raider** with a horned brazier helm, ember-furnace backpack, and heavy magma-cleaver gauntlets.

## 3. Astral Stalker (`artilian:astral_stalker`)
- **Biome / Structure:** Starlight Highlands — **Starlight Watchtower**
- **Elements / Hitbox:** `STARLIGHT / SHADOW` (`0.85 × 1.85`, `eyeHeight = 1.55F`, `shadowRadius = 0.48F`)
- **Silhouette Archetype:** Lithe, hooded **Twilight-Slate & Nebula-Cloak Watchtower Phantom** with a crescent starlight crown, tattered astral mantle cutouts, and twin starlight wrist-blades.
