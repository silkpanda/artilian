# Pet drawing notes

The kids' direction for each drawing, recorded when the drawing was filed.
Phase 2 Stage 5 pets (Shadow Wisp, Cinder Crag, Zaphox, Galecorn) and Stage 6 pets (Prismling, Flurrywing, Sunbeam, Frogduck, and Zapkin) are documented in `PLAN.md`.

## steve-pets-drawing-2026-11-15.jpg

- **Shadow Creature (working name: Shadow Wisp):** Dark charcoal-violet blocky head with shadowy face speckles and glowing violet/magenta eyes, atop a tapered purple V-shaped torso with a glowing center core, two spindly purple arms each ending in 3 splayed claw tendrils, and two long curved trailing purple legs.
- **Fire / Rock Creature (working name: Cinder Crag):** Squat crimson-stone & molten-orange body housing angled fiery eyes and a wide arched magma mouth in the middle of the torso, a flat stone brow across the top crowned by 3 pointy flame spires (two larger outer flames + one smaller center flame), standing on two blocky stone pillar legs.

## alex-pets-drawing-2026-11-15.jpg

- **Zaphox:** Cheetah-fox-elephant fusion (blue-outlined circular face in original sketch -> cheetah spots/tear marks, fox muzzle/ears, curling elephant mini-trunk, and jagged orange sawtooth lightning wings along its back) with four teal/cyan legs.
- **Galecorn:** Yellow fox-unicorn fusion with a bushy tail ("uses its tail to blow things away"), four slender legs, and a shimmering teal-outlined head/horn.

## lightning-guy-drawing-2026-11-17.jpg

## frog-duck-drawing-2026-11-17.jpg

- A frog duck. One single character.
- **Frog Duck:**
  - The teal body is the frog. Its face is in the middle of the body: brown eyes, two teal dots, and the brown cloud shape below them.
  - The orange shape on top of its head is the duck. It is part of the same creature.
  - Four legs with clawed feet.
