# Dragon Species Anatomy & Silhouette References (Phase 5 Stages 4–5)

Compiled 2026-11-22 as the design reference for Phase 5 dragon species so each species has a distinct 3D silhouette (limb count, wing structure, posture, and tail/head profile) rather than a palette swap.

## 12 Reference Dragon Archetypes

| # | Archetype | Limbs & Wings | Silhouette & Anatomical Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Classic Western Dragon** | 4 legs + 2 separate wings (6 limbs) | Heavy-set quadruped torso, long arched neck, horned skull, separate dorsal wings, spiked tail. |
| 2 | **True Wyvern** | 2 hind legs + 2 wing-arms (4 limbs) | Bipedal raptor/bat stance; no separate forelegs—wings double as clawed forelimbs; lean torso and barbed/spaded stinger tail. |
| 3 | **Drake** | 4 heavy legs, 0 wings | Flightless, low-slung crocodilian/monitor-lizard silhouette with heavy armor plates and thick tail. |
| 4 | **Amphithere (Amphiptere)** | 0 legs + 2 large wings | Winged sky-serpent: long undulating S-curved body, zero legs, sweeping glider/feathered wings and fanned tail plume. |
| 5 | **Lindworm** | 2 clawed forelegs, 0 wings | Serpentine body with a pair of muscular front arms right behind the head; burrower/ambush posture. |
| 6 | **Wyrm / Guivre** | 0 legs, 0 wings | Pure colossal coiled serpent silhouette; relies on body coils and horned/fanged head. |
| 7 | **Eastern Dragon (Lung / Ryū)** | 4 short legs, 0 wings | Very long sinuous serpentine body, flowing mane/crest, whiskered/antlered head; swims through the air. |
| 8 | **Hydra** | 4 heavy legs, 0 wings | Bulky low quadruped with multiple branching necks and independent heads. |
| 9 | **Feathered Serpent (Quetzalcoatl)** | 0 legs (or 2 wings) | Plumage-ringed serpent with layered feather-fan collar, crest, and tail plume. |
| 10 | **Sea Serpent / Leviathan** | 0 legs, paddle fins | Fin-crested aquatic serpent with lateral pectoral fins and wide caudal fluke. |
| 11 | **Kirin / Qilin** | 4 hooved legs, 0 wings | Deer-like chimeric dragon body with antlers, scaled flanks, and flowing flame mane. |
| 12 | **Knucker** | 4 short legs or 2 wings, amphibious | Low-slung cavern/pool drake with webbed frills and broad jaw. |
